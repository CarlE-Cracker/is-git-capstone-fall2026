##Ingredients:
• All-purpose flour: 2 1/4 cups
• Baking soda: 1 teaspoon
• Salt: 1/2 to 1 teaspoon (use 1/2 tsp if using salted butter, 1 tsp if using unsalted butter)
• Butter: 1 cup (2 sticks), softened to room temperature
• Granulated white sugar: 3/4 cup
• Packed brown sugar: 3/4 cup (light or dark brown sugar)
• Vanilla extract: 1 to 2 teaspoons
• Eggs: 2 large eggs
  expresso powder(powder for baking not making coffee amount unknown) 1 teaspoon 
• Chocolate chips: 2 cups (about 12 ounces) semi-sweet chocolate morsels
